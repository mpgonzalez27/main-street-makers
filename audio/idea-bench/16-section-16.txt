[calm][warm] Founder Notebook Builder. Write your Founder Sentence. A Founder Sentence explains the heart of a business idea in one clear line. It has three parts: who you help, what you offer, and why it helps. Here is the pattern: "I can help blank by offering blank because blank." Nora might write, "I can help readers by offering sturdy bookmarks because scraps of paper fall out of books." Try more than one sentence. "I can help neighbors by watering porch plants because plants get thirsty when families are busy." "I can help gift givers by offering handmade birthday cards because cheerful cards make people feel remembered." A clear Founder Sentence is like a tiny map. It does not answer every business question, but it points you in the right direction.
