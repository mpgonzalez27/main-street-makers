[calm][warm] Competition Corner. Other choices are not enemies. Competition means customers have other choices. A customer can buy from your booth, buy from another booth, make the item at home, or decide not to buy anything today. Competition is not always bad. It teaches founders. If another booth has a long line, ask why. Is the sign clear? Is the table easy to understand? Is the price fair? Is the product especially useful? A business can be different in honest ways. It can be faster, clearer, kinder, safer, prettier, sturdier, more personal, or easier to understand. Different does not mean strange. Different means there is a clear reason the customer might choose it. A founder should not copy another person's work. A founder can learn from what customers like, then make an honest offer with their own ideas, own design, and own promise.
