[calm][warm] Customer Watch. Customers are real people. A customer is a person, family, group, or business that might want or need the offer. A business is not made for "everybody." It is made for particular people with particular needs. If you sell pet treats, your customers might be families who have dogs. If you sell bookmarks, your customers might be readers at co-op, grandparents who give books as gifts, or kids who like colorful supplies. Good founders picture the customer clearly. What does the customer care about? What problem is the customer trying to solve? What would make the customer smile and say, "Thank you, that helped me"? A customer is never just a money machine. A customer is a neighbor to serve honestly. A strong business treats customers with respect, tells the truth, and keeps promises.
