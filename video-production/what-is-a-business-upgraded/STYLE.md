# What Is a Business? — Upgraded Hybrid Animation Style Guide

SAVE Learning Co. / Main Street Makers  
Ticket: SAV-35

## Goal

Create a richer second-pass explainer that feels less like a slide deck and more like a warm animated story, while keeping exact text, equations, and teaching diagrams safe and readable.

## Visual Style

Main Street Press animated storybook:

- cozy small-town flyer
- warm paper texture
- cartoon-realistic props
- gentle board-game depth
- shopkeeper ledger details
- rubber-stamp teaching moments
- simple, sheltered homeschool setting

## Safety and Licensing

- Original AI-generated illustrations only.
- No stock photos.
- No modern brand logos.
- No protected book covers or packaging.
- No identifiable real people.
- No dating, occult, horror, crude humor, politics, or controversial content.
- Children can be simplified illustrated characters, seen from behind or in soft generic form.

## Hybrid Method

Use AI video for richer story scenes:

- cozy book club table
- child loses page
- Nora sketches bookmarks
- bookmark basket appears
- customer focus scene
- wise next choices

Use programmatic cards for exact teaching screens:

- Need → Offer
- Product definition
- Expense = money out
- Choose a fair price
- Revenue = money in
- Profit equation
- Founder Notebook
