[warm][curious] Imagine you are at a cozy homeschool book club.

Children are sitting with chapter books. Some books are open on laps. Some are stacked in a basket. One reader keeps flipping pages back and forth.

She says, “Oh no. I lost my place again.”

Nora notices.

That is where a business idea can begin.

A business starts when someone notices a need.

A need is something that would make life better, easier, cleaner, safer, faster, or more joyful.

In this story, the need is simple. Readers need a way to save their place in a book.

Nora thinks, “I could make bookmarks.”

That is her offer.
