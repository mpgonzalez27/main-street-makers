[warm][clear] An offer is what a business gives to a customer. It might be a product. A product is a thing people can buy. It might be a service. A service is a helpful job people can buy.

Nora’s bookmarks are products.

She calls her little business Nora’s Bookmark Basket.

Now Nora has to think like a founder.

A founder is a person who starts something.

First, Nora thinks about her customers. Her customers are the people who may need or want her bookmarks.

She does not say, “Everyone in the whole world is my customer.”

That would be too big.

She starts small.

Her first customers are children at book club who read chapter books.

Next, Nora thinks about supplies.

She needs cardstock, ribbon, glue, and colored pencils.

Supplies cost money before she sells anything.

That money is called an expense.

An expense is money the business spends.

Nora spends four coins on supplies.

Then she makes eight bookmarks.

Now she has to choose a price.

A price is the amount a customer pays.

If the price is too high, customers may say, “No thank you.”

If the price is too low, Nora may not have enough money to replace her supplies.

Nora chooses one coin for each bookmark.
